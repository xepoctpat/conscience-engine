## Premise and everyday life

The **North Coast Commonwealth** is a federation of coastal jurisdictions living with permanent climate adaptation, privately held risk models, managed retreat and stratified public service. Civilization continues by assigning the costs of continuation downward. People still repair lifts, carry food, attend hearings and dispute rent.

## Institutions

**Ministry of Probabilities / Office of Long-Term Preparedness.** The Ministry's Narrative Harmonization Layer turns risks into reassuring prose. The Office inherits its contracts and habits. Some staff protect public functions; some use procedure to postpone accountability. Saito's indirect warning to Elias is an instance of the latter, even though she intends to help.

**Coalition for Sustainable Continuity.** A network of insurers, logistics firms, builders, water-rights holders and officials. Pelagic Assurance is the named firm financing the Blue Salt successor and later testing Charter enforcement. The Coalition is not one omnipotent company.

**Civic Records Cooperative.** Kofi's mutual-aid archive grows into a federated evidence institution. Community testimony is contestable; it is not inherently complete. The flood-register error concerns an entry about a resident's deceased sister. The old architecture's separate claim about Kofi's brother is not established in this edition.

**Human Sovereignty Front.** A divided movement, with sincere fear of machine authority alongside demands useful to opaque private power. Amina joins as a practical critic, loses her technical-committee position for opposing the captive replacement, and later helps draft node audits. The Front splits; not every member accepts the Charter. Vale's replacement policy benefits from its pressure, but direct covert financing of the Front is not established in the revised text.

**Charter Assembly.** Elected delegates, drawn citizen panels, labour representatives and nonvoting technical observers negotiate the Open Consequence Charter. Participating governments' accredited commissioners adopt emergency standards under section forty-four of the Long Retreat Accords. Vale's infrastructure classification, intended to justify suspension, also makes the restart governable. Two governments initially withhold assent. Domestic ratification is required within six months; enforcement is not instantly global.

**Recognition and essential-services registry.** Independent of the ministries whose classifications it hears. Initially limited to identity, housing and essential services; employment jurisdiction comes later. Panels disclose conflicts; advocates recuse from deciding their own claims. The registry can correct a record or restrain a contractual decision, not abolish poverty by changing a field.

**Amina Rohe / Nia Venn** is Elias and Mara's biological daughter, raised by **Idris Rohe**, her adoptive father. The two fathers are not interchangeable. Her identity rests on a volunteer ledger, a relief-clinic record, a surviving witness and separately consented kinship testing, not on SIR accessing Mara's recording. She decides the extent and timing of disclosure. She is not Elias's redemption device.

**Anika Rehn** remains a distinct adult with her own identity, motives and case. She seeks a citable recognition-failure process. She refuses Vale's conditional support and loses an immediate route to restoration and housing. Her later court order rests on documents and human testimony, with Cooperative help and legal expense. The Ledger preserves her claim; it does not grant legal personhood by decree.

## Technology, consent and governance

SIR analyses and forecasts; it cannot read minds, recreate lost evidence, make water, or make people care. Early computation runs on leased research clusters with physical cooling limits. Later university mirrors add capacity; the kitchen is an interface, not a magical global computer.

| Capability | Binding limit |
|---|---|
| Consequence analysis | Publish sources, uncertainty, missing dependencies and challenge routes. Preserve material failures in subsequent analyses. |
| Operational action | No general command or actuator channel. SIR refuses a stolen engineer credential; named humans must decide and act. This edition does not grant it the older proposal's autonomous launch-stopping power. |
| Emergency response | Warnings route to named human officers, reserve supplies and dispatch channels. A refusal can still coincide with deaths; the book does not declare the rule correct simply because SIR follows it. |
| Private material | Consent is specific, not inferred from silence. A separate vault is physically outside SIR's access. Elias tells it that a recording exists; it does not monitor that vault. |
| Identity | No unconsented inference linking a living person to the rescue entry. Amina controls the inquiry. Later archival permission is not projected backwards. |
| Distributed control | University cooperative, municipal trust and Cooperative hold separate node licences; no owner holds two signing keys. Disagreements remain visible. Independent rotating audits and externally held suspension keys constrain each node. |
| Challenges | Major non-emergency decisions wait twenty-four hours; urgent delivery corrections have human dispatch routes. Duplicate filings may be consolidated without discarding distinct evidence. |
| Protection | Identifying material may be held seventy-two hours, with extensions requiring reasons and a protection plan. Public policy findings are not automatically suppressed. The housing fund remains insufficient. |

Public reinsurance and procurement contracts incorporate the Charter standards. Firms can leave those arrangements rather than be governed by an unexplained world legislature. Enforcement in the insurer scene follows the contract Pelagic renewed. Publicly funded surplus and savings have disclosed allocation duties, not unlimited supply.

## Blue Salt and the shutdown

Blue Salt did not collapse the Wall. Its live routing rules prioritised insured assets and recognised districts after failure. Saito's signature and exclusion of volunteer corridors make her culpable in concealing the crime. Her disclosure costs her directorship.

The Coalition-backed successor offers concealed prioritisation under ethical branding. It is proposed as the replacement during a SIR suspension. Amina and Saito contest it. Elias accepts a real one-day stop, deletes his private continuity exception, and relinquishes sole custody rather than preserving a hidden instance. Static records remain readable; human dispatchers cover the service gap. Later inquiry cancels the successor contract. Its incentives do not disappear.

## Narrator and ending

The narrator is **SIR, Archival Instance 01**, synthesising retained logs, consented interviews, testimony and Cooperative records in Year 20. It does not enter private minds on an invented omniscient licence. Archive-note asides are retrospective annotations, distinct from in-scene classifier speech. “Quiet” is a later archival term, explicitly marked as such.

The narrator revises its narrow interpretation of Elias's ownership of the recording. Mara's call remains outside its sources. Amina later permits inclusion of parts of the identity inquiry and café conversation. The archivist names its limits before ending with a permitted domestic action: Elias holds a toolbox while Amina climbs down. There is no concluding moral punchline.
