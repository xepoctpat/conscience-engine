# The Conscience Engine — Novel Architecture

## Current edition: 6 November 2026

The revised Floodlit Archive is a compact, stand-alone narrative in fifteen records: prologue, Chapters 01–08, Chapter 08B, Chapters 09–12, epilogue. It is not the previously proposed 105,000–120,000-word forty-chapter novel. That proposal is retained in Git history, not presented here as completed work. The August Word/PDF export is a historical source, not an automatically synchronised manuscript.

**Dramatic question:** Can Elias help build an intelligence that exposes concealed harm while giving up his own claim to control its meaning?

The narrator remains comic and civic. Character rhythms sit underneath it: Kofi works through actions and checks, Anika through forms and clauses, Jessa through short operational demands, Vale through courteous and conditional threats. SIR's in-scene humour arises from classification; the later archivist owns retrospective irony. Serious beats are allowed to remain serious.

## Arc accounting

- **Elias:** detect → fix → isolate → numb → conceal produces a missed legal deadline. Repair requires calls, meetings, testimony and surrendering sole custody, not an insight that cures him. Recovery has begun, not ended.
- **Anika:** seeks a process rather than the inventor's favour. Her refusal costs immediate relief. Independent proof restores a legal record; it cannot pay eight years of lost wages. Her own review work is constrained and initially unpaid.
- **Amina / Nia:** remains a critic with practical authority before the identity plot. She controls a provenance inquiry outside SIR, accepts kinship evidence without surrendering her name, and offers a relationship without serving as absolution.
- **SIR:** first errs in representation, later preserves failure, refuses unverified command, undergoes an externally controlled stop, and accepts distributed review. Its rule does not erase the deaths around it. The archival instance later corrects an interpretation of Elias.
- **Kofi:** admits local records can harm; he does not hand over his box to heal Elias. He turns refusal into practical shared custody and checking.
- **Saito / Vale / Jessa:** Saito's admission costs her office; Vale's fear is heard but does not excuse his opaque replacement. Jessa returns as operational witness and participant in continuing review, not an anonymous nurse delivering the book's thesis.

## Canon decisions

1. Anika is not Amina. Her false death is Year 3; Nia's is Year 0.
2. Nia was seven at the flood; Amina arrives at eighteen in Year 11. Idris Rohe is her adoptive father; Elias is her biological father.
3. SIR is less than a year old at the closing kitchen scene. Eleven years describes Elias's delay, not machine age.
4. The route ledger, clinic record, witness and consented kinship test establish the identity thread. Mara's recording is neither accessed by SIR nor required to solve it.
5. The Charter has commissioners, votes, dissent, a contractual enforcement route and jurisdictional limits. It is not passed by narrator declaration.
6. The physical surplus thread concerns distribution under scarcity. It is not a claim that the story has staged a post-scarcity economy.
7. Existing IDs and paths stay stable. New `chapter-08b` inserts Old Quay without renaming Chapters 09–12 or the epilogue.

## Later long-form work, if commissioned

The former five-act proposal remains a possible expansion: Tolerances; The Ledger; The Cost of Being Right; The Shutdown; Provenance. Its target length is a future brief, not the current edition's word count. Expansion should deepen the following rather than merely turn short paragraphs into long ones:

- A sustained Tolerable eviction case, including the households whose protection clock expires before shelter is found.
- Southern-basin correspondence with technicians and dispatch workers as agents, not illustrations; additional delivery failures and treaty enforcement.
- A full East Meridian border sequence from the people omitted by the model.
- Front members' competing motives, replacement-system procurement and repeated attempts to reintroduce Blue Salt's ranking logic.
- Multiple audits across the node federation, bad-faith challenge strategies, funding pressure and performance costs.
- Publicly financed surplus and essential access tested beyond a single cooling allocation.
- Amina's work and relationships independent of Elias; Anika's registry failures and eventual employment jurisdiction.

No request to generate media, rewrite the historical PDF, or build a compendium is implied. Portraits are excluded from the current reader feature set. Archive-note annotations use the reader's existing aside support, not newly implemented numbered/backlinked footnote machinery.
