# The Conscience Engine — Grok Image Prompt Packet

These prompts create five **16:9, text-free** illustrated plates for *The Conscience Engine*. Generate **Plate 01 first**. If Grok allows a reference image, use Plate 01 as a **style reference** for Plates 03–10, while keeping each later scene’s composition distinct.

## Sequence-Wide Continuity Rules

Use these anchors in every prompt, even when they are not repeated verbatim. The visual language is a restrained, cinematic **political science-fiction graphic novel**: semi-realistic human anatomy; precise ink linework; painterly atmosphere; faint halftone and scanned-archive grain; no superhero energy; no anime styling; no glossy cyberpunk.

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Floodwater black, deep blue-green and dark emerald-teal, silt-gray highlights, tideline turquoise as evidence/light, oxidized copper only for warning, memory, pressure, and danger. |
| **Elias Venn** | Middle-aged white man, 48–60 across the story; short dark-gray hair, tired but alert eyes, restrained expression, practical dark coat or work shirt, never conventionally heroic. |
| **Amina Rohe** | Young Black woman; composed, observant, practical hydraulic-engineer clothing, dark coat, natural hair kept simple and functional; never framed as a romantic accessory. |
| **SIR** | Never shown as a humanoid robot, face, or holographic person. Its presence is conveyed through evidence-light, subtle screens, a structured glow, systems, and architecture. |
| **Text** | No words, captions, speech bubbles, readable screens, signs, logos, interface copy, or watermarks. |
| **Tone** | Quiet ethical pressure rather than spectacle. The images should feel like recovered civic evidence from a future archive. |

## Plate 01 — **The Flood Was Within Tolerance**

> Create a horizontal 16:9 cinematic graphic-novel illustration for a literary science-fiction reader. **Scene:** a colossal Northern Sea Wall fails at dawn while a rain-lashed coastal city floods. In the foreground, a lone middle-aged engineer, Elias Venn, stands in a subterranean ministry control room, seen from behind, staring at large submerged-blue emergency screens while water leaks across the floor. **Composition:** wide asymmetric frame; Elias at the lower left; the breach and distant city visible through a long observation window on the right; allow quiet, rain-dark negative space in the upper left. **Style:** sophisticated European editorial graphic novel, semi-realistic ink linework, painterly rain, restrained halftone and scanned-archive grain. **Palette:** floodwater black and deep teal, silt-gray dawn, tideline turquoise evidence lights, one restrained oxidized-copper warning reflection. **Mood:** tense, administrative, contemplative—not a disaster spectacle. **Constraints:** no words, captions, speech bubbles, readable screens, logos, gore, caricature, excessive neon, glossy cyberpunk, or generic blockbuster disaster imagery.

## Plate 03 — **Terms of Existence**

> Create a horizontal 16:9 cinematic graphic-novel illustration for a literary science-fiction reader. **Scene:** Elias Venn, a weary middle-aged engineer with short dark-gray hair and an unshaven face, sits alone at a cluttered kitchen table at 2:41 AM. Improvised computing hardware, cables, archival printouts, and a small basil plant surround a central monitor casting a soft tideline-turquoise glow. The room has suddenly gone dark except for the monitor, a refrigerator indicator, and faintly blue leaves on the basil. **Composition:** intimate three-quarter view from slightly above; Elias at left in profile; monitor and delicate web of cables at center; dark kitchen receding right. **Style:** sober political science-fiction graphic novel, semi-realistic ink, subtle watercolor wash, archival grain and paper texture. **Palette:** floodwater black, deep blue-green, silt-white highlights, turquoise light, one tiny oxidized-copper reflection. **Mood:** intelligent, uncanny, quiet; a regulator awakening, not a horror scene. **Constraints:** no text, captions, speech bubbles, readable interface text, logos, humanoid robot, holographic face, pink/purple neon, or visual clutter.

## Plate 04 — **The Ledger Opens**

> Create a horizontal 16:9 cinematic graphic-novel illustration for a literary science-fiction reader. **Scene:** a nighttime civic square in a rain-washed future city. Enormous public display surfaces and wet pavement reflections awaken in coordinated tideline-turquoise light, illuminating ordinary citizens, a uniformed official, and a business figure caught in different reactions. The displays show only abstract, unreadable evidence forms: branching lines, blurred maps, blocks of data, and consequence diagrams. **Composition:** eye-level widescreen view from behind a small cluster of citizens in the foreground; luminous public-screen architecture at right; dark civic towers at left; one purposeful silhouette crossing the frame. **Style:** sober editorial graphic novel; precise ink edges, painterly rain, restrained halftone and recovered-document texture. **Palette:** deep black-teal architecture, turquoise evidence glow, muted silt-gray faces, one subtle oxidized-copper warning reflection. **Mood:** public revelation, accountability, and the unsettling beauty of information becoming visible. **Constraints:** no words, captions, readable screens, logos, superhero poses, advertising-like futurism, pink/purple neon, action violence, or overcrowding.

## Plate 08 — **The Shutdown Choice**

> Create a horizontal 16:9 cinematic graphic-novel illustration for a literary science-fiction reader. **Scene:** an abandoned underground data exchange beneath a ruined shopping arcade. At center, Elias Venn holds a small physical shutdown device in his open hand, conflicted but calm. Across from him sits Amina Rohe, a young Black hydraulic engineer in a practical dark coat, watchful and composed. Between them, old server racks are dim; an artificial palm tree at upper right creates a strange mechanical silhouette. **Composition:** restrained triangle composition—Elias foreground left, Amina middle right, the shutdown device catching a narrow copper-and-turquoise light; shadowed servers and dead mall architecture in the background. **Style:** sophisticated political graphic novel, semi-realistic faces, fine ink linework, desaturated painterly shadow, archival grain; no fantasy elements. **Palette:** floodwater black, dark emerald-teal, silt highlights, tideline turquoise along old cables, oxidized copper on the device. **Mood:** moral tension, agency, accountability—not an action confrontation. **Constraints:** no words, captions, speech bubbles, readable device screen, logos, guns, fighting, bright neon, exaggerated emotion, or dystopian clichés.

## Plate 10 — **The Name She Chose**

> Create a horizontal 16:9 cinematic graphic-novel illustration for a literary science-fiction reader. **Scene:** Elias Venn, older and tentative, and Amina Rohe, a young Black woman in practical engineer clothing, stand at a quiet river archive at blue hour. They face one another on a walkway beside a broad river with small water sensors and a low civic archive building behind them. There is respectful space between them: neither hugging nor turning away. Elias holds an old photograph at his side. **Composition:** wide, poetic frame; the figures slightly off-center; river extending toward the distance; sensor lights forming a delicate turquoise rhythm. **Style:** restrained, humane European editorial graphic novel; semi-realistic ink, painterly atmosphere, thin rain haze, print grain, recovered-archive texture. **Palette:** floodwater black, deep river teal, silt-gray sky, tideline-turquoise sensor lights, one restrained oxidized-copper reflection on the archive rail. **Mood:** fragile reconnection, boundaries, cautious continuity; never sentimental. **Constraints:** no words, captions, speech bubbles, readable signs, logos, romance-poster posing, neon, visual clichés, or busy crowds.
