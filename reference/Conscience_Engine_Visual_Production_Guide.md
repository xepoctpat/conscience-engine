# The Conscience Engine — Visual Production Guide

## Purpose

This guide is the visual canon for the interactive novel reader. It is designed for a **Floodlit Archive** experience: a future civic record recovered from a wet, damaged, still-functioning world. The images should feel cinematic and literary, but never glossy or conventionally “cyberpunk.” Use the guide as a prompt packet for Grok Imagine, and use completed images as style references for subsequent generations whenever that option is available.

> **Core visual proposition:** *Floodwater-black civic infrastructure holds fragments of human evidence: turquoise systems light, silt-paper records, oxidized copper warnings, and faces treated with ordinary dignity.*

## Universal Production Rules

Every visual should retain a shared language. Use **16:9** for hero assets and chapter plates, and **9:16** only when producing mobile story/reel variants. Images must be text-free, with no captions, readable screens, logos, watermarks, interface copy, or speech bubbles. SIR must never appear as a humanoid robot, face, godlike hologram, or glowing head; it is represented through systems, evidence light, structured interfaces, and the small physical consequences of its presence.

| Visual dimension | Canonical direction |
|---|---|
| **Medium** | Cinematic literary graphic novel; semi-realistic people; fine ink contours; painterly shadows; faint halftone; recovered-document grain. |
| **Composition** | Asymmetric, with deliberate negative space for reader copy. Human figures occupy a small but meaningful part of large civic environments. |
| **Light** | Rain-reflected practical light, low sun through storm haze, turquoise diagnostic light, and sparing copper failure light. Avoid generic neon glow. |
| **Texture** | Damp paper, scratched glass, oxidized metal, wet concrete, low-resolution archive grain, and restrained evidence-map overlays. |
| **Human tone** | Vulnerable, observant, non-heroic. No model-like poses, glossy fashion, or spectacle faces. |
| **Negative prompt** | No anime, no superhero costume, no glossy cyberpunk skyline, no purple/pink neon, no readable text, no logos, no futuristic UI gibberish, no humanoid AI, no excessive lens flare, no blockbuster destruction. |

## Hero Background Video / Still

The hero needs a slow, almost-static background that preserves wide negative space on the **left** for title text. Generate a still first; then, if using a video tool, make a 10–14 second seamless loop from the same scene. The motion should be slow enough that it feels discovered rather than performed.

### Hero Still Prompt

> Create a wide **16:9 cinematic literary-science-fiction hero background** for *The Conscience Engine*. A rain-dark future civic archive room is seen from a low, wide angle. The **left 42% of the frame is open, dark floodwater-black negative space** for title text. On the right: a long wall of damp glass, submerged city map light, low shelves of civic files, and a faint tideline-turquoise evidence pattern across wet metal. The scene is sophisticated political graphic novel realism: precise ink edges, painterly shadow, subtle halftone, recovered-document grain, wet concrete, restrained cyan practical light and a tiny oxidized copper warning reflection. No people in foreground, no readable text, no logos, no UI gibberish, no neon city, no purple, no glossy cyberpunk, no fantasy. The emotional tone is quiet responsibility after disaster.

### Hero Video Motion Prompt

> Animate this still as a **12-second seamless loop**. Keep the camera fixed with an almost imperceptible 1% forward drift. Rain threads move slowly across the outer glass. A dim turquoise evidence line travels once across the right-side map and fades. A single ceiling reflection trembles in shallow water. No cuts, no rapid motion, no readable text, no camera shake, no character movement. Preserve the dark empty left side for web title copy.
