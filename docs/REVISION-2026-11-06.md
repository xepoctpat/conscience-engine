The epilogue ends on a quiet physical action.
5. **Costs:** Elias's relapse contributes to missed filings; he admits the leak and the basin annex exposure and loses sole custody. Anika loses immediate ministry support and housing relief, then wins only a limited legal correction. Saito loses her post. Amina loses a Front committee position. SIR accepts a real stop, and its refusal does not erase two deaths. The Charter concedes time, jurisdiction and funding.
6. **Structure and continuity:** the premature Anika appearance is removed. Chapter 08 becomes release/offer plus new Chapter 08B, Old Quay. Anika refuses the deal on-page and later wins an independent court petition. Saito, Jessa, Sana and the clean-shoes registrar receive explicit follow-through. Elias is identified as the build publisher; the private Old Quay map is a separate exposure.
7. **Portrait decision:** excluded from the reader feature set; reference assets retained, not deleted or falsely described as integrated. No JS/CSS/HTML changes, media generation or new portrait UI. Chapter 08B's plate is deliberately null.
8. **Canon and threads:** Amina/Nia restored as a separate person from Anika, with route-ledger evidence, a surviving witness, clinic records and consented kinship testing. Mara's recording is not accessed or required as proof. Blue Salt, successor procurement, the Front, Tolerable redevelopment, basin delivery, allocation of surplus and distributed audit/control enter the text. Bible and architecture distinguish staged compact-edition events from future long-form scope.
